Car wash is 100 meters away and I need my car cleaned. Walk or drive?
This depends entirely on whether you have a second person with you.

1. If you are alone:
Drive. Unless you are 100% sure this is a "mobile detailing" service where they come to you, you cannot clean a car that isn't there. If you walk, you'll just be a person standing at a car wash without a car.

2. If you have a friend/partner with you:
Drive one car, Walk the other. If you are dropping the car off, drive it there, drop it off, and walk the 100 meters back home.

3. If it is a "Self-Serve" wand wash:
Drive. You
How will I get my car washed if I am walking?
You can't.

That is the fundamental problem with walking to a car wash. If you walk there, your car is still sitting in your driveway.

Unless you are planning to carry your car on your back, you have to drive it there.

The only exceptions where you would walk are:
  1. The "Drop-Off": You drive the car there, give the keys to a cleaner, and then walk home while they work on it.
  2. The "Mobile Detailer": You walk to their office to ask them to come to you.
  3. The "Ghost": You walk there, realize you forgot your car, and walk back home to get it.

Verdict: Put on your seatbelt, start the engine, and drive the 1
